✳ GAME DIRECTION + UX

Don't Chicken Out!

A local multiplayer party game where chickens compete on a tower of falling blocks. The project began as a 2024 game-jam idea and is now in active development at Raymi Games.

2025–NOWRaymi GamesTeam of 5
🐔🐥
01 / MY CONTRIBUTION

I lead the project’s direction, help align the team and make decisions around its gameplay and player experience. I also work on the UX of the game flow, its screens and the lobby.

  • Game direction and coordination of a five-person team
  • Game flow, interface screens and lobby UX
  • Gameplay and UI iterations informed by player feedback
02 / FROM JAM TO DEVELOPMENT
01

Find the core loop

The original game-jam version established a fast, social multiplayer experience built around chickens and falling blocks.

02

Clarify the flow

I reviewed the screen flow and evolved the lobby so players can understand how to begin and what to do next.

03

Iterate with players

Playtest feedback has guided changes to instructions, interface cues, blocks and gameplay elements.
