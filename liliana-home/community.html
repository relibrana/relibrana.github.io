The community side of my practice: building accessible spaces, connecting emerging game developers and turning opportunities into shared momentum.

SANDA / 2024—NOW

Building the conditions for people to join in.

SANDA is a nonprofit community founded in Peru in February 2024. It helps emerging game developers navigate scattered information, meet peers in a closer and more human way, and access talks, workshops, showcases, game jams, scholarships and networking opportunities.

Pixel Play was SANDA’s first initiative. I lead ideation and event planning, venue search, sponsors and partnerships, studio outreach, volunteer coordination, marketing, budget and logistics. In Peru I lead most operational work with the events team; local representatives lead the corresponding work in their countries.

Developer surveys are private. They ask whether events supported wishlists, playtests, new-mechanic feedback, followers and community feedback; no private responses or causal results are published here.

PIXEL PLAY / 2026—NOW

It started in Lima, Peru. Now it follows a regional route

Pixel Play grew from local showcases in Lima into a route designed with local communities across Latin America. Each country adapts the format to its own audience, venue and ecosystem.
